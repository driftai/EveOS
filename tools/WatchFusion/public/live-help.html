Link a playing tab

One-time setup in desktop Chrome or Edge:

  1. Open the browser's Extensions page and turn on Developer mode.
  2. Choose Load unpacked and select EveOS/tools/WatchFusion/browser-extension.
  3. Pin WatchFusion Media Link to the browser toolbar.

In Find Media, choose Link a playing tab and copy its private pairing link. Open the tab playing your media, click the companion, paste the link, and choose Connect this tab. Only access to your WatchFusion server is requested; source access lasts for the tab you explicitly choose.

The companion sends a cropped view of the visible video area plus that tab's audio. Keep the source video in view within the tab; the tab itself may be in the background. YouTube playlist changes keep flowing. Cross-origin navigation may require reconnecting. Embedded players inside another site's iframe and protected video may not provide a usable picture. Full desktop/window sharing is never substituted.

Music Library

Start a group in EveOS Music Library, then select Connect Audioflix in WatchFusion. Local files and browser-playable audio use Audioflix's existing audio graph. Queue selection, play/pause, previous/next, seek, speed, volume, shuffle and loop stay connected to the same Music Library queue. A provider iframe that cannot expose its audio needs the tab companion instead.

Rooms

Create a room before or after connecting the source. Guests open the normal room link and press Listen here if their browser needs a tap for sound. Only the host can control the linked source. Keep the source tab or EveOS open while sharing. Stop link ends the stream for everyone; it does not delete the room.

LAN peers connect directly. Across different networks, the server operator may need to configure WATCHFUSION_ICE_SERVERS with a STUN/TURN service. A Cloudflare HTTP tunnel alone does not relay WebRTC media.
